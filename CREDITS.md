# Credits & third-party icon attributions

Sticky Notes is released under the MIT License. Some UI icons are third-party
works under their own licences, listed below. Icons under **CC BY** require the
attribution given here; MIT icons retain their authors' notices; CC0 / Public
Domain icons need no attribution and are credited as courtesy.

Most icons come from [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com/).

## Modifications

This project renders icons **recoloured to match the active theme** (icons are
tinted to the note/UI ink at draw time), and some source SVGs were additionally
**edited (e.g. colours changed)** before inclusion. Such icons are therefore
derivative works of their originals. For the CC BY icons listed below, this note
satisfies the licence's requirement to indicate that changes were made.
